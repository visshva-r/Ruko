# RUKO — 90-second demo video script

**Theme:** Digital Safety & Cybersecurity (HackNowa Global Hackathon 2026)

| Time | Visual | Voiceover |
|------|--------|-----------|
| 0:00–0:12 | Phone screen: fake WhatsApp “Maa, naya number, ₹8,000, don't call” | “Every day, families in India lose money to messages that sound like their son or daughter. One wrong UPI transfer is enough.” |
| 0:12–0:35 | Open RUKO live site. Click demo **Son — new number, don't call** | “RUKO means stop. Paste or upload any message. In seconds you get Stop, Caution, or Safe — not a vague chatbot answer.” |
| 0:35–0:55 | Point at **STOP**, risk score, highlighted quotes, **Safe action** | “See the proof on the message: don't call, unknown UPI, urgent money. One clear action: call the saved number, don't pay on chat.” |
| 0:55–1:05 | Tap **Listen** or show Hindi toggle + **Copy reply** | “Replies in English and Hindi, plus a line you can read aloud to a parent.” |
| 1:05–1:15 | Click demo **Safe — real family check-in** → **SAFE** | “RUKO isn't an alarm that always screams scam. Real messages can be safe.” |
| 1:15–1:30 | Quick flash of code or footer: rules + Gemini | “Under the hood: a rule engine for UPI, OTP, and urgency, merged with Gemini for screenshots and context. Built solo for HackNowa — RUKO, stop scams before you pay.” |

**Recording tips:**

- 1080p, show cursor on highlights, keep background music low or off.
- **Listen / सुनें:** Browser speech often does not appear in the mic-only track. In OBS, enable desktop/browser audio, or read the spoken line yourself on the microphone.
- **Copy reply:** Click once and hold the frame on **Copied!** / **कॉपी हो गया!** (stays ~4.5 seconds).
- **Live paste vs demo:** Use demo buttons for instant STOP/SAFE; for one live paste say “now checking with rules + Gemini” during the 1–2s **Checking…** state.
